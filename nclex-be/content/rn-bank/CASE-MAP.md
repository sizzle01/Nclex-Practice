# Case-study conversion: topic map (draft for review)

Source: the 1,910 questions now in the bank (main plus wave 2 chunks 6 to 8), sorted under the
topics in the nursing-school review book. Target after conversion: about 85% of questions inside
full six-question case studies, 15% standalone.

## Shape of every case (NCLEX-hard)

| # | Clinical judgment step | What the question makes the candidate do | Typical format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognize cues | Pick the findings that need follow-up from nurses' notes, vital signs, labs, ABG, rhythm description | highlight text or table |
| 2 | Analyze cues | Link each finding to the condition it fits, or explain what a lab or ABG means | matrix, drop-down |
| 3 | Prioritize hypotheses | Name the condition the client is most at risk for, and the evidence for it | drop-down rationale |
| 4 | Generate solutions | Sort the prescriptions: indicated, not indicated, or contraindicated, with drug knowledge such as hold parameters, interactions and antidotes | matrix, multiple response |
| 5 | Take action | New data arrives; choose the actions now, in order, including dose calculations | multiple response, ordering, calculation |
| 6 | Evaluate outcomes | Later data in a trend table; decide what improved, what did not, and what comes next | matrix, trend table |

Rules:
- Every case unfolds. Questions 4 to 6 bring new data, so the candidate has to re-judge.
- Pharmacology is tested inside the disease: mechanism, hold parameters, toxicity signs, antidotes, interactions and monitoring labs.
- Fundamentals topics (ABG, ABO, EKG, dosage calculation, IV fluids, electrolytes, labs) and head-to-toe assessment and cranial nerves appear as case data, not as recall questions.
- Difficulty: at least half difficult, the rest moderate, none easy. Wrong options are near misses that a partly prepared candidate would pick.

## Standalone 15% (about 290 questions)

Bowtie and trend questions, plus hard multi-client priority and delegation questions. A single-client case cannot test which of four clients to see first, but the real exam does test it.

## Chunks

- Each chunk is about 16 cases plus 4 standalone, which is 100 questions.
- Med-Surg takes 13 chunks. Cardiac comes first, including pacemaker, hemodynamics and shock, then pulmonary, endocrine, and so on.
- The 26 existing cases are reviewed and count toward these targets.
