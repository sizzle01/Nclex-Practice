Transcript: lung sounds, posterior lung bases (4 seconds)

Two breaths are heard through a stethoscope placed on the back, over the bases of the lungs.

Breath 1 (0 to 2 seconds): air moves in and out. Near the end of breathing in, short, fine crackling sounds are heard, like strands of hair rubbed together beside the ear.

Breath 2 (2 to 4 seconds): the same fine crackles are heard again at the end of breathing in. Breathing out is quiet.

No wheezing and no harsh, low-pitched sounds are heard.
